The game graph contains selected public motor-vehicle road classes. Edge endpoints
follow the source OSM topology and intermediate shape points are simplified.
The visitor approaches in ../vn-access/ are simulated alignments. Their junctions
use this road graph. The generator uses OSM coastlines, building footprints and
water geometry together with the game's elevation raster to place the roads.
Mapped traffic controls and supported turn restrictions are packaged separately
in ../vn-traffic/. Signal timing is simulated. Live traffic and temporary
closures are outside this road dataset.
The HUST landmark adds campus service lanes from the OSM API for simulated
driving, including visitor- and staff-access roads. Real access rules vary.
The overview and place search are derived from the same snapshot.
