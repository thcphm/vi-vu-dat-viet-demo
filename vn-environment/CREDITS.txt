The game averages the reported residents across a 4×4 window of native 3-arc-second
cells, stepping two cells at a time. The result is expressed in residents per native
cell (~85–93 m wide in mainland Vietnam). Cells without a published count contribute
no residents to the window. A window with no published counts is recorded as unknown.
Values are logarithmically quantised for compact offline distribution.
Traffic and pedestrian activity are game simulations driven by these estimates.

The game resamples the published categorical overview from the 10 m WorldCover
map to 1.5 arc seconds (~43–46 m) using nearest-neighbour resampling. Class codes
follow WorldCover, including 10 tree cover, 20 shrubland, 30 grassland, 40 cropland,
50 built-up, 60 bare/sparse vegetation, 70 snow/ice, 80 permanent water bodies,
90 herbaceous wetland, 95 mangroves and 100 moss/lichen.

Half-degree tiles cover the regions crossed by roads in the bundled road index.
Coordinate system: WGS 84. Nodata in either layer is retained independently.
Rebuild with tools/build_vn_environment.py using the source release above.
